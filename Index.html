Personal — not for anyone else's eyes

The Kit

For when the pressure builds and you need somewhere for it to go besides out at a person, or nowhere at all.

Not suppression. Not explosion. Feel it, then move it. Name it for a breath, then reach for whatever fits where you are and how much time you've got.
Filter by where you are
In the moment under 2 min · anywhere

Name it, silently

One word, no story. "Angry." "Offended." "Hurt." Let it register in your body for one breath before you do anything else. This alone lowers the charge.

Discharge, in place

Wall push-offs, fast air punches, shake your limbs out like flinging off water. Spend the adrenaline, don't wait to calm down first.

Cold water

Wrists, face. Blunts the physiological spike fast — it's a real reset button, not just a distraction.

Hold the tears at the brim

Breathe slowly through your nose, unfocus your eyes for a second, press your tongue to the roof of your mouth. You're still feeling it — you're just managing the output for the room you're in.

Clench and release

Full-body tension for 10 seconds, then drop it. A few rounds. Works seated, silent, in the dark, next to someone asleep.

Vocal & expressive 5–20 min

Sing it loud and ugly

Not performance-pretty. You already have the instrument — use it as a tool on purpose, not just for joy.

Scream it out

Into a pillow, or in the car with the windows up. Full volume, no audience.

Growl low

Humming or growling in your chest — releases the tension without needing volume. Good for quiet hours or public places.

Dance until you're not composed

No choreography. Move until the charge actually drops, not just until you feel silly for starting.

Ugly journal dump

Ballpoint pen, no punctuation, no rereading. Get it out of your body and onto the page — this isn't for anyone, including future you.

Fast walk

Around the block, around the store. Good for when you need to physically leave a space, not just sit in it.

The bigger containers longer / planned

Working out

The main one. Something to push against — weights especially. Actually metabolizes the cortisol spike, not just distracts from it.

Shadow boxing / hit a pillow

Targeted release, built for anger specifically. Gives the impulse somewhere to land that isn't a person.

Self-pleasure

Full unrestrained release, on your own terms, always available. Not the only valve — but a real one.

Diamond painting or building

The come-down, after the charge is already spent. Occupies your hands, or channels the energy into something architectural — very you.

Weighted pressure

Weighted blanket, or your own arms wrapped tight around yourself. Deep pressure calms the nervous system directly.

Cognitive redirect stuck somewhere, can't move

Count backward by 7s

From 100. Occupies the analytical brain just enough to break the loop without becoming another bypass.

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you feel. Useful when the anger tips into overwhelm rather than staying contained.

Feeling it isn't the danger. It's information.
